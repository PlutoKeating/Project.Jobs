Before you launch, let a whole town try it.

Hand Jobs a URL. Two hundred very different people live in JobsWorld. They get on with their own days, come across your product in the app store, in ads and from friends, and decide for themselves whether to look, to use it, to stay and whom to tell.

curl -fsSL https://jobs.plutokeating.beer/install | bash

You need a machine with Docker. Once it is up, open the web page and connect your own model provider. Read the script first

After you launch, they decide

Paste a URL, a name and one line; a dev server on your own machine works too. Nobody is assigned to test. Rules work out who comes across it and who notices. Whether to open it, how to use it and whom to tell is up to them.

The screen is all they can see

Someone on the subway with one free hand, someone minding a child, someone with five minutes left: each uses it in their own here and now. All they are handed is what is on the screen, and they do not know it is a test.

Read one plain page

Whether it works, what hurt most, who stays and who leaves, why people who saw it did not open it, and the one thing to fix first. Every sentence opens to who said it, their words and the screen at that moment.

In your terminal and your coding agent too

Launch as soon as the code is written, pull back the conclusion and a list of issues with steps to reproduce, and keep going. You can also walk into the same town in Terraria, or connect it to Feishu.

# launch, check on it, get the conclusion
jobsworld publish http://127.0.0.1:3000/ \
  --name "Quick Notes" --tagline "Write it down the moment you think of it"
jobsworld status "Quick Notes"
jobsworld report "Quick Notes" --eng

# add it to Claude Code (any agent that speaks MCP works the same way)
claude mcp add jobs -- jobsworld mcp

It all runs on your machine

The web page, the command line, coding agents, Feishu and Terraria are five ways into the same capabilities. Only two things reach outside: your product, and the model providers you connect.

  • Your data stays put

    Residents, memories, screenshots and conclusions live in Docker volumes on your machine and never pass through our servers.

  • You bring the models

    Pick a provider in the web page, paste a key, tick the models. What you have spent stays in the page header.

  • Open source

    Released under AGPL-3.0, with the source on GitHub.
